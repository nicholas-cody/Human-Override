OUR MISSION

Build the guardrails before
we need the emergency brake.

Human Override is an advocacy movement for practical rules that preserve human authority in an increasingly automated world. We want technologists, workers, parents, policymakers, creators, business owners and everyday people at the same table.

We are not asking society to abandon innovation. We are asking society to decide—deliberately—where automation belongs, where it does not, and what protections must exist before deployment.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Lines worth drawing.

01

HUMAN AUTHORITY

High-impact decisions need meaningful human review and a real path to appeal.

02

ACCOUNTABILITY FIRST

Organizations must remain responsible for automated outcomes.

03

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

People should know when AI creates, ranks, rejects, monitors or communicates.

04

HUMAN DIGNITY

No one should be trapped where no responsible human can intervene.

05

PURPOSE OVER PROFIT

Work provides mastery, community, independence and purpose—not just output.

06

FREEDOM OF CHOICE

Technology should leave people more capable and independent.
