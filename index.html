01 — WHY THIS MATTERS

Progress needs a purpose.

Efficiency cannot be the only measure of a future worth building.

A society can automate more tasks and make faster decisions—and still lose something essential. Human dignity, useful work, judgment, relationships and responsibility cannot simply become inefficiencies to engineer away.

The question is not whether AI will become more capable. The question is what we choose to preserve as uniquely human, and who gets to make that choice.

02 — THE MISSION

Build the guardrails before we need the emergency brake.

Human Override is an advocacy movement for practical rules that preserve human authority in an increasingly automated world. We want technologists, workers, parents, policymakers, creators, business owners and everyday people at the same table.

We are not asking society to abandon innovation. We are asking society to decide—deliberately—where automation belongs, where it does not, and what protections must exist before deployment.
